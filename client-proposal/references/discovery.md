# Discovery

Goal: enough information to price the work and to know whether you want it.

## Ask in this order

**1. The outcome**
- What should this website do for your business in six months?
- What happens today without it? What do you use now?
- How will you know it worked?

**2. The people**
- Who uses it, and who decides? (find the real decision maker)
- Who will answer questions during the build, and how fast?

**3. The content**
- Who writes the texts and takes the photos? Are they ready?
- Do you have a logo, colours, brand guide?
- Arabic, English, or both? (this alone changes the design and the estimate)

**4. The features**
- Which three screens or features matter most?
- Anything you have seen elsewhere that you want it to behave like?
- What must it connect to (payments, WhatsApp, CRM, delivery, ERP)?

**5. The constraints**
- Deadline, and why that date?
- Hosting and domain: do you own them?
- Who maintains it after launch?

**6. Money**
Ask last, and as a choice - never as "what's your budget":
> Projects like this usually land in one of three ranges: X, Y or Z. Which feels
> closest? (If none, tell me the ceiling and I will tell you what fits.)

## Signals to record

| Signal | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| "just a simple website" + 30 features | scope explosion incoming |
| no decision maker in the call | proposal will be forwarded, not read |
| content "we'll send later" | timeline risk - needs an assumption clause |
| wants a copy of a famous site | expectations risk - name the gap in writing |
| asks price in the first message | they are comparing, not buying - qualify fast |
| pays deposit without questions | good client, deliver well |

## Stop condition

You can write the scope, the assumptions and a timeline without guessing.
If you cannot, you are missing one of: content, decision maker, integrations,
or deadline.
