# Terms

Plain-language clauses that prevent 90% of freelance disputes. Adapt to the client's
market and legal context; this is not legal advice.

## Payment

- Deposit before work starts; no deposit, no start date.
- Balance before handover: code, repository, hosting, and credentials transfer on
  final payment, not before.
- Late payment: work pauses after N days, and the delivery date moves by the delay.
- Accepted methods and who pays transfer fees: state it.

## Scope changes

- Any request outside `scope.md` is a change request: written, estimated, approved,
  then scheduled. It does not enter the current sprint by talking about it.
- Two revision rounds per screen are included. A third round is billed.

## Client responsibilities

- Final content (text, images, logo) by the agreed date.
- One decision maker who consolidates feedback.
- Accounts for third-party services (domain, payment, SMS) opened in the client's name.
- Feedback within N working days; silence for more than N days pauses the timeline.

## Delays

- If content or approval is late, the timeline shifts by the same number of days.
- If the project is paused for more than 30 days, a restart fee may apply.

## Ownership and licences

- On final payment the client owns the custom code and design.
- Third-party libraries keep their licences.
- The freelancer may show the work in a portfolio unless the client asks otherwise
  in writing.
- Fonts and stock images must be licensed by the client or accounted for separately.

## Support and warranty

- 30 days of bug fixing after handover (bugs, not new features).
- After that: retainer or hourly.
- Hosting, domain, and third-party subscriptions are the client's cost, not included.

## Cancellation

- Work done to date is billed at the agreed rate; the deposit covers the first
  phase and is non-refundable once that phase starts.

## Confidentiality

- Client data and credentials are not shared, reused, or kept after the project
  except where needed for support.

## What to never promise

- "It will never break."
- "Unlimited changes."
- "Google first page."
- "I will fix anything forever for free."
